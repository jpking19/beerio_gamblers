Frequently Asked Questions

What is Beerio Gamblers?

Beerio Gamblers is a friendly betting game built around Mario Kart races. Players place wagers before each race and watch the results live — the more chaotic the race, the more fun the bets.

How do I start a game?

Hit the Start New Game button above, set up your players, and assign bet amounts before each race begins. The game tracks everything automatically so you can focus on racing (and drinking).

How are bets calculated?

Each player picks a racer and bets a set number of tokens. Payouts are determined by finishing position — first place pays out the most, while last place loses their stake. Side bets on specific outcomes (blue shell, longest drift, etc.) are also supported.

Is this for real money?

Nope! Beerio Gamblers uses a virtual token system purely for fun. No real money ever changes hands — just bragging rights and maybe a round of drinks.

What's the drinking component?

The classic Beerio Kart rule applies: you must finish your drink before you cross the finish line, but you can't drink while the kart is moving. Gambling adds an extra layer — the player who loses the most tokens each round takes a penalty sip. Always drink responsibly and play within your limits.
